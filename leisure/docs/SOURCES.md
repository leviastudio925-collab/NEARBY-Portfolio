- Blender product: original editable lift-box model adapted with FOLLOW / STAY / HEIGHT labels, a tea cup, coaster and book; existing cabinet geometry retained.
- Balcony tea, sofa reading and room-to-room following scenes: AI-generated concept simulations using the project's model appearance as a visual reference. They are not photographs of a deployed product or proof of successful autonomous following.
- Figma floor plans: editable vector room layers, adapted with an enclosed balcony chair and A / B / C parking points. Mapping, saved places and boundaries are proposed UI behaviors.
- Original prototype photography and Blender workspace screenshot remain process evidence; earlier labels and objects may appear in those historical images.

No user interviews, survey percentages, clinical evidence, motion accuracy, load capacity or spill-prevention results were fabricated.
